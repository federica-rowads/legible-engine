Frequently asked questions

Is the Stride GTS good for wide or flat feet?

Yes — it's built specifically for overpronation and flat feet. It comes in four widths (Narrow, Standard, Wide, X-Wide), and the dual-rail medial support resists arch collapse without an aggressive medial post. Flat-arched or wide-forefoot runners usually choose the Wide or X-Wide.

How long is the break-in period?

Minimal. The compression-molded EVA midsole and gusseted knit upper are tuned to feel ready out of the box, so most runners report little to no break-in. We suggest one or two easy runs before a long or hard effort.

What's the return policy?

Free 60-day returns. Run in them on the road — if they aren't right, send them back within 60 days for a full refund, even if they're worn. Return shipping is free in the US.

Does it fit true to size?

It runs about a half size short. If you're between sizes or buying for a wider foot, size up half a size and pick the matching width.
